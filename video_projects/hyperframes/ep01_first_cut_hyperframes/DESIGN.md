# 《炼狱酒馆 EP01》HyperFrames Visual Identity

## Style Prompt

Dark cyber tavern short-video edit with warm amber bar light, purple neon accents, clear anime/chibi character readability, and no extra overlay text. The composition should preserve the SeedDance clips as the visual source of truth and only provide timeline control plus audio sync.

## Colors

- Background black: `#05030a`
- Tavern amber: `#ffb347`
- Neon purple: `#b84cff`
- Text white: `#f8f5ff`
- Safe shadow: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65)`

## Typography

- System sans-serif stack for any debug labels only: `Microsoft YaHei`, `Noto Sans CJK SC`, `Arial`, sans-serif.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not add new subtitles over the baked-in text.
- Do not add new characters, logos, watermarks, or explanatory cards.
- Do not stretch video clips.
- Do not add large decorative overlays that cover faces or baked subtitles.
